1. Who we are

Family Foodie Book (familyfoodiebook.com) is a free website for keeping and sharing recipes with your family and with everyone. It's an independent project. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Family Foodie Book, and "you" means anyone who visits the site or has an account.

This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Using the site is also covered by our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, and the name you choose to show on your recipes and comments.
  • Your password: your browser sends it straight to our sign-in provider, Amazon Cognito, over an encrypted connection. It never reaches our own servers or database, and we can't see it. Cognito stores it in a protected form, not as plain text.
  • What you post: recipes (titles, descriptions, ingredients, steps and other details you enter) and comments.
  • Families: the families you create or join, their names, when you joined, who owns each family, and the family's invite code.
  • Appearance settings: your chosen season theme, light or dark mode, font, text size, decorations and accent colors. When you're signed in, these are saved to your account so they follow you between devices.

Location (only if you turn it on)

The appearance settings can match the seasons to your hemisphere. If you choose to detect it from your device, your browser asks your permission first. Your device's position is used only inside your browser to work out whether you're in the Northern or Southern Hemisphere. We keep only that answer ("north" or "south"). Your actual location is never sent to us or stored.

Information collected automatically

  • Request logs: when your browser talks to our API, we log the time, your IP address, which feature was used (for example "list recipes"), whether it worked, and how long it took. We use these logs to keep the site running and secure.
  • Error details: if something goes wrong, technical details about the error are logged so we can fix it.
  • Abuse protection: to stop the site being flooded, we count how many requests are made in each short time window. For signed-in members we count by account. For signed-out visitors we count by IP address. These counters are deleted automatically (see How long we keep it).

We don't use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools, and we don't build profiles of your interests.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account: signing you in, keeping you signed in, and resetting your password.
  • To show recipes and comments to the right people, based on the visibility you choose.
  • To run families: invite codes, member lists and family recipes.
  • To send emails about your account, such as sign-up confirmation codes and password resets. We don't send marketing email.
  • To protect the site and its members: preventing abuse, enforcing our Terms of Service, and investigating problems.
  • To fix bugs and keep the site working well.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, share it for advertising, or use your recipes and comments to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. Who can see your recipes and comments

  • Public recipes can be seen by anyone on the internet, including people who aren't signed in. Your display name appears on them.
  • Family recipes can be seen only by you and the current members of the families you shared them with. You can share a family recipe with one family or several. Members of each family can see which of their own families it's shared with, but not the names of your other families. Only you can see every family it's shared with.
  • Comments can be seen by anyone who can see the recipe they're on, along with your display name. If a family recipe is shared with several families, members of all of them can see all its comments.
  • Family member lists show each member's display name and when they joined. The family's owner can also see its invite code. Anyone who has a family's invite code or link can join that family, so share it only with people you trust.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members or visitors.

If you share a family recipe to the public, it becomes a public recipe that anyone can see. If you leave a family, or the family's owner removes you, recipes you shared with that family stay visible to its members until you stop sharing them with it or delete them.

5. Companies that help run the site

The site runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS). AWS hosts the website, stores our database, runs sign-in through Amazon Cognito, and sends our account emails. AWS processes information on our behalf and under its own privacy and security commitments. Your information is stored in the United States.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the safety, rights or property of our members, the public or us, or to investigate fraud or abuse. If the site is ever transferred to someone else, your information would go with it, and the new owner would have to respect this policy or tell you about changes first.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use cookies. The site uses your browser's own storage for a few things it needs:

  • Sign-in: the tokens that keep you signed in are kept in your browser. Normally they're cleared when you close the tab. If you tick "Keep me signed in on this device", they're kept on the device until you sign out. Signing out removes them.
  • Appearance settings: kept on your device so the site looks right as soon as it loads.
  • The site's own files: copies of its pages, styles, scripts and icons, so it opens quickly, and without a connection when you've installed it as an app. These copies hold no recipes, comments or account details. Clearing your browser's data for the site removes them.
  • Sign-up progress: while you confirm your email, your email address and name are kept in the tab so you don't have to type them again. They're cleared when the tab closes.

Because we don't track you or sell data, there's nothing for "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals to turn off.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, recipes, comments and families: for as long as your account exists, or until you delete them.
  • Recipes and comments you delete: removed from our database straight away. Deleting a recipe also deletes the comments on it.
  • Request and error logs: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Abuse-protection counters: set to expire two minutes after they're created. The database then deletes them automatically, usually within a few days.
  • Families: when the last member leaves, the family and its invite code are deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

You can do these yourself on the site:

  • Change your display name on your profile.
  • Edit or delete your recipes, change who can see them, and delete your comments.
  • Leave any family.
  • Change your appearance settings, or turn location detection off.

For anything else, email support@familyfoodiebook.com from the address on your account:

  • Get a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information you can't change yourself, such as your email address.
  • Delete your account. We'll delete your account, profile, recipes, comments and family memberships. If you own a family, ownership passes to its longest-standing member.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may also give you the right to object to or limit how we use your information, or to move it to another service. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live, and we won't treat you differently for making one. We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm the request comes from you, usually by replying to the email address on your account.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority, though we'd appreciate the chance to help first.

9. Legal bases (EU and UK visitors)

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we use your information:

  • to provide the service you signed up for (your account, recipes, comments and families);
  • for our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure, preventing abuse and fixing problems (request logs, error details and abuse protection);
  • with your consent, for location-based hemisphere detection, which you can turn off at any time.

Your information is stored and processed in the United States.

10. Children

Family Foodie Book isn't meant for children under 13, and they may not create an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email support@familyfoodiebook.com and we'll delete it. Members aged 13 to 17 need a parent's or guardian's permission, as our Terms of Service explain.

11. Security

The whole site is served over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are handled only by Amazon Cognito. Each request to our API is checked so that people see only what they're allowed to, and the site blocks content from other websites from running on its pages. No website can promise perfect security, though, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else. If you think someone else has accessed your account, reset your password and let us know.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we'll give notice on the site before it takes effect.
